Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Crumble does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your scores, goals, settings and any game in progress are saved only on your own device, so you can pick up where you left off. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) deletes them.

iCloud

On iPhone, Crumble also keeps a backup of your progress (best score, stats, goals, unlocked looks, Adventure stars, Daily and Challenge results, but not a game in progress) in your own iCloud account, so a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account picks it up. It is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, only you can reach it, and we never receive it. You can turn iCloud off for Crumble in your iPhone's Settings.

Game Center

On iPhone, Crumble can post your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center if you are signed in to it. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive your Game Center information. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Children

Crumble is made to be played by families, including children. Because it collects nothing, there is no personal information from children (or anyone else) for us to hold, share or sell.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first and the date above will change with it.
