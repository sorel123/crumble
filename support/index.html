How to play

Drag the three shapes onto the board. Fill a whole row or column and it crumbles away. Clear lines one after another to build a combo, and empty the whole board for a clean sweep. The game ends when none of your shapes fit.

Questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Close the app at any time and your game will be waiting. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do leaderboards and achievements work?

On iPhone, sign in to Game Center (Settings, Game Center) and your best scores and goals are posted automatically. Game Center is optional; the game plays the same without it.

How do I turn the sound or music off?

Tap the speaker button at the top of the game, or open Settings for music and sound options.

Can I change how it looks?

Yes. Settings has looks for the blocks and the board, and you unlock more by reaching goals.

Does it cost anything?

No. Crumble is free to play.
